<h1>Contact SLS Helpdesk</h1>
<p></p>
<h4>Login or Account-Related Issues</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For login or account-related issues, you may refer to the <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/login-troubleshooting/authentication/index/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Login Troubleshooting Tips</a>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>You may also <a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/login-troubleshooting/authentication/reset-sls-password-student/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">reset your password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the SLS login page</a> if
you have previously set up a password-reset email address.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>If the issue cannot be resolved through self-reset, please contact your
<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/login-troubleshooting/get-help/get-help-from-your-school/" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">School-based Helpline</a>for assistance.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4>Other Technical Issues</h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>For all other technical issues, you may contact SLS Helpdesk.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Please note that call waiting times may be longer during peak periods,
such as Home-Based Learning days.</p>
</li>
</ul>
