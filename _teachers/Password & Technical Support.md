<h4>Browser Extensions</h4>
<hr>
<p>If you are using a supported browser, but see a message that your browser
is unsupported, you may be using a browser extension that interferes with
browser detection.</p>
<p>Try disabling your browser extensions and see if the problem is resolved.</p>
<p>Refer to the guides below on how to disable extensions in your particular
browser:</p>
<p><a href="https://support.google.com/chrome_webstore/answer/2664769" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Chrome</a>
</p>
<p><a href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT203051" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Safari</a>
</p>
<p><a href="https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/disable-or-remove-add-ons" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Firefox</a>
</p>
<h4>Cookies and Javascript</h4>
<hr>
<p>With all browsers, cookies and JavaScript should be enabled.</p>
<h4>Cache and Browsing History</h4>
<hr>
<p>If you encounter any login issues, you will need to clear your cache and
browsing history.</p>
<p>You may refer to this&nbsp;<a href="https://www.learning.moe.edu.sg/files/Login%20Troubleshooting/Clear-Cache.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">guide</a>&nbsp;on
how to clear your cache.</p>
<h4>Slow Site Loading</h4>
<hr>
<p>If it takes a long time to load a page on SLS, try the following:</p>
<ol data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Check that your internet connection is stable.</strong>&nbsp;Visit
other websites to confirm whether you have internet connectivity issues.
If the problem persists, you may wish to seek assistance from:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Your school's Desktop Engineer (DE) (if the problem occurs in school)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Internet Service Provider (ISP) (if the problem occurs at home)</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p>Ensure that your web browser has been updated to the latest version.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Clear your browser's&nbsp;<a href="https://www.wikihow.com/Clear-Your-Browser%27s-Cache" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">cache</a>&nbsp;and&nbsp;
<a href="https://www.wikihow.com/Clear-Your-Browser%27s-Cookies" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">cookies</a>&nbsp;and restart your web browser.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Switch to another web browser that is supported.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<p>If the problem persists, please contact the SLS Helpdesk at <a href="mailto:helpdesk@sls.ufinity.com" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">helpdesk@sls.ufinity.com</a> or
call 6702 6513.</p>
